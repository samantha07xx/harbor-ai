# Finding Primary Care in Ontario

## Summary

Many newcomers in Ontario may not have a family doctor or nurse practitioner when they first arrive. This document explains common ways to look for primary care and what users can say when contacting clinics or services.

For Harbor AI MVP, this document supports non-emergency healthcare navigation and communication preparation. It does not guarantee that a provider is accepting new patients.

## What Primary Care Means

Primary care is usually the first point of contact for non-emergency health concerns.

Primary care may be provided by:

- Family doctors
- Nurse practitioners
- Family health teams
- Community health centres
- Other primary care clinics

A primary care clinician can help with ongoing health needs, prescriptions, routine checkups, referrals, and follow-up care.

## Ways to Find Primary Care

### Health Care Connect

Health Care Connect is an Ontario program that helps people who do not have a family doctor or nurse practitioner look for one.

Users may need to provide basic information and may be connected with a provider if one is available.

### CPSO Doctor Search

The College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario provides a doctor search tool. Users can search for doctors in Ontario and check basic public information.

This can help users identify doctors or clinics they may want to contact.

### College of Nurses of Ontario Registry

The College of Nurses of Ontario provides a public registry for nurses, including nurse practitioners.

This can help users verify nurse practitioner registration information.

### Calling Local Clinics

Some users may contact local clinics directly to ask whether they are accepting new patients.

Users can prepare a short script before calling.

## What Newcomers Should Know

- It may take time to find a family doctor or nurse practitioner.
- Not every clinic is accepting new patients.
- Walk-in clinics may help with some non-emergency issues while the user is still looking for ongoing care.
- Health811 can help users find health advice or services.
- Users should avoid sharing unnecessary personal health information before confirming what the clinic needs.

## Useful Phrases

- I am looking for a family doctor or nurse practitioner.
- Are you accepting new patients?
- I am new to Ontario and do not have a family doctor yet.
- Could you tell me how to register as a new patient?
- Do I need an Ontario health card to register?
- Is there a waitlist I can join?
- Can you repeat that more slowly, please?
- Could you write down the next steps for me?

## Information to Prepare Before Calling

Users may want to prepare:

- Full name
- Phone number
- Preferred language
- General location or postal code
- Whether they have an Ontario health card
- Whether they are looking for ongoing primary care
- Any accessibility or communication needs

Users should not provide sensitive information unless the clinic or service clearly requires it.

## Safety Notes

This document is for healthcare navigation support only.

Harbor AI should not promise that a user will be matched with a provider. It should not recommend a specific doctor as medically better than another. It should help users understand options and prepare communication.

For urgent or life-threatening symptoms, users should call 911 or seek emergency care.
