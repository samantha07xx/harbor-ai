# Care Options in Ontario

## Summary

Ontario newcomers may need different types of care depending on whether the health concern is urgent, non-emergency, ongoing, or related to general health advice.

For Harbor AI MVP, this document focuses on non-emergency primary care communication. It helps users understand common care options before choosing where to go.

## Main Care Options

### Family Doctor or Nurse Practitioner

A family doctor or nurse practitioner is usually the main primary care clinician for new or ongoing non-emergency health concerns.

They can help with:

- Common illnesses and injuries
- Prescriptions
- Routine checkups
- Chronic condition management
- Referrals to specialists
- Follow-up care

### Walk-in Clinic

A walk-in clinic may be used when the user has a non-emergency health concern and cannot see a family doctor or nurse practitioner soon enough.

Walk-in clinics may help with common issues such as minor illness, minor injury, prescription renewal questions, or basic medical assessment.

Users should check clinic hours, fees, and whether they need a health card before visiting.

### Health811

Health811 is an Ontario service that can help people get health advice or find health services.

Users can contact Health811 when they are unsure what kind of care they may need, but it should not replace emergency services.

### Emergency Department or 911

For serious, life-threatening, or urgent symptoms, users should call 911 or go to an emergency department.

Harbor AI should not help users decide that an emergency symptom is safe to ignore.

## What Newcomers Should Know

- Primary care is for non-emergency health concerns.
- A family doctor or nurse practitioner can provide ongoing care.
- Walk-in clinics can help when users need care but do not have timely access to their regular clinician.
- Health811 can help users find health advice or services.
- Emergency situations should be handled by 911 or emergency departments, not by AI tools.

## Useful Phrases

- I am looking for non-emergency medical care.
- I do not have a family doctor yet.
- I would like to know if this clinic accepts walk-in patients.
- Do I need to bring my health card?
- Can you help me understand where I should go for this concern?
- I need help describing my symptoms in English.

## Safety Notes

Harbor AI does not provide diagnosis, treatment decisions, or emergency triage.

If the user reports severe chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, loss of consciousness, stroke-like symptoms, or another urgent concern, the system should direct them to call 911 or seek emergency care immediately.
