# OHIP Basics

## Summary

OHIP is Ontario's public health insurance plan. Many newcomers need to understand what OHIP is, what a health card is, and what questions to ask before receiving care.

For Harbor AI MVP, this document provides basic communication support about OHIP and health card questions. It does not determine whether a user is eligible for OHIP and does not provide legal or billing advice.

## What OHIP Is

OHIP stands for Ontario Health Insurance Plan.

It helps cover many medically necessary health care services for eligible Ontario residents.

Users usually show an Ontario health card when accessing insured health services.

## What a Health Card Is

A health card is a government-issued card that shows a person is registered for OHIP.

Clinics, hospitals, and health care providers may ask for a health card before providing insured services.

Users should bring their health card to medical appointments if they have one.

## Applying for OHIP

Newcomers may need to apply for OHIP after arriving in Ontario if they meet eligibility requirements.

The application process may require identity documents, proof of residency, and other documents depending on the user's situation.

Users should check the official Ontario.ca OHIP page for the most current eligibility and document requirements.

## What OHIP May Cover

OHIP may cover many medically necessary services, such as visits to doctors and some hospital services.

However, OHIP does not cover everything. Some services, medications, forms, tests, or uninsured clinic services may involve additional costs.

Users should ask the clinic or provider if they are unsure whether a service is covered.

## If the User Does Not Have OHIP

A newcomer may not have OHIP yet, may be waiting for coverage, or may not be eligible.

In that case, the user should ask about:

- Whether the clinic accepts patients without OHIP
- The cost of the visit
- Payment methods
- Whether any documents are required
- Whether there are community health services available

Harbor AI should help the user ask clear questions, not decide eligibility or cost.

## Useful Phrases

- I have an Ontario health card.
- I do not have OHIP yet.
- I am waiting for my health card.
- Do you accept patients without OHIP?
- How much does the visit cost if I do not have OHIP?
- Is this service covered by OHIP?
- What documents should I bring?
- Can you explain the fee before the appointment?

## What Newcomers Should Bring

If available, users may bring:

- Ontario health card
- Government-issued photo ID
- Immigration or residency documents
- Private insurance information
- Student insurance information
- Medication list
- Previous medical documents, if relevant

The exact documents needed may depend on the clinic, service, and user's situation.

## Safety Notes

This document is for general healthcare communication support only.

Harbor AI should not determine OHIP eligibility, promise coverage, or provide legal advice about immigration or insurance status.

For urgent or emergency symptoms, users should seek immediate medical care even if they are unsure about insurance coverage.
