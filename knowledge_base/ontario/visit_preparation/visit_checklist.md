# Visit Checklist

## Summary

Preparing before a primary care visit can help newcomers communicate more clearly, reduce anxiety, and avoid forgetting important details.

For Harbor AI MVP, this document supports the Prepare flow. It helps users organize information before a walk-in clinic visit or a family doctor / nurse practitioner appointment.

## What to Bring

Users may want to bring:

- Ontario health card, if available
- Government-issued photo ID
- Private insurance or student insurance information, if available
- List of current medications
- Allergy information
- Previous medical documents, if relevant
- Notes about symptoms
- Phone with translation or communication support, if needed

The exact documents needed may depend on the clinic and the user's situation.

## Symptom Notes to Prepare

Before the visit, users should prepare simple notes about:

- Main symptom or concern
- When it started
- Where it is located
- How severe it is
- Whether it is getting better, worse, or staying the same
- What makes it better or worse
- Related symptoms
- Medication already taken
- Allergies
- Existing medical conditions
- Pregnancy status, if relevant
- Recent travel or exposure, if relevant

These notes can help the user answer common questions during the visit.

## Questions the Clinician May Ask

A doctor, nurse practitioner, or clinic staff may ask:

- What brings you in today?
- When did the symptom start?
- Where do you feel the symptom?
- How severe is it?
- Have you had this before?
- Are you taking any medications?
- Do you have any allergies?
- Do you have a family doctor?
- Do you have an Ontario health card?
- Have you tried anything for this problem?

## Questions the User May Want to Ask

Users may want to ask:

- What do you think may be causing this?
- Do I need any tests?
- Do I need medication?
- Are there symptoms I should watch for?
- When should I come back or seek urgent care?
- Can you explain the instructions in simpler words?
- Can you write down the medication instructions?
- Do I need a follow-up appointment?
- Is this covered by OHIP or will there be a fee?

## Before Leaving the Visit

Before leaving, users should try to understand:

- What the clinician thinks the next step is
- Whether medication is needed
- How to take medication
- Whether tests are needed
- Where and when to get tests
- When to follow up
- What warning signs require urgent care
- Who to contact if symptoms change

## Useful Phrases

- I wrote down my symptoms so I do not forget.
- Can I show you my symptom notes?
- I am not sure how to describe this in English.
- Could you explain that in simpler words?
- Could you please write down the instructions?
- What should I do if the symptoms get worse?
- When should I come back?
- Is there anything I should avoid?

## Safety Notes

Harbor AI can help users prepare information and questions, but it should not decide what tests, medications, or treatments are needed.

If the user has urgent or severe symptoms, the system should direct them to call 911 or seek emergency care instead of relying on AI preparation.
