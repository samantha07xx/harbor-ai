# Symptom Communication

## Summary

Newcomers may know what they feel but may not know how to describe symptoms clearly in English. This document helps users prepare simple, structured symptom descriptions for non-emergency primary care visits.

For Harbor AI MVP, this document supports the Prepare and Live Assist flows. It helps users organize what to say, but it does not interpret symptoms or provide diagnosis.

## Basic Symptom Structure

A clear symptom description usually includes:

- What the symptom is
- Where it is located
- When it started
- How severe it is
- Whether it is constant or comes and goes
- Whether it is getting better or worse
- What makes it better or worse
- Related symptoms
- Medication or home care already tried
- Relevant medical history

## Describing Time

Users can say:

- It started this morning.
- It started yesterday.
- It started three days ago.
- I have had this for about one week.
- It comes and goes.
- It is constant.
- It is getting worse.
- It is getting better.
- It has not changed.

## Describing Location

Users can say:

- The pain is in my stomach.
- The pain is on the left side.
- The pain is on the right side.
- It is in my chest.
- It is in my throat.
- It is in my lower back.
- It spreads to my arm.
- It stays in one place.

## Describing Severity

Users can use a simple 1 to 10 scale:

- The pain is mild.
- The pain is moderate.
- The pain is severe.
- On a scale from 1 to 10, it is about a 6.
- It is uncomfortable but not severe.
- It is strong enough that I cannot sleep.
- It is strong enough that I cannot work or study.

## Describing Related Symptoms

Users can mention related symptoms such as:

- Fever
- Cough
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Diarrhea
- Dizziness
- Headache
- Shortness of breath
- Rash
- Fatigue
- Swelling
- Bleeding

Users should describe what they actually experience and avoid guessing the diagnosis.

## Medication, Allergies, and Medical History

Users may need to say:

- I took pain medication.
- I took cold medicine.
- I have not taken any medication.
- I am allergic to penicillin.
- I do not know if I have any allergies.
- I take medication every day.
- I have diabetes.
- I have high blood pressure.
- I am pregnant.
- I had surgery before.

## Useful Phrases

- I am not sure how to describe this symptom.
- I feel pain here.
- The pain started three days ago.
- It gets worse when I eat.
- It gets better when I rest.
- I have not had this before.
- I had something similar before.
- I am worried because it is getting worse.
- Can you help me understand what information you need?

## Example Symptom Summary

Example:

```text
I have had stomach pain for three days. The pain is in the middle of my stomach. It comes and goes, but it feels worse after eating. On a scale from 1 to 10, it is about a 6. I also feel nauseous. I have not taken any medication yet.
```

This type of summary can help the user communicate clearly during a primary care visit.

## Safety Notes

Harbor AI should not use symptom descriptions to diagnose the user.

If the user describes severe chest pain, trouble breathing, signs of stroke, heavy bleeding, loss of consciousness, or another urgent symptom, the system should direct the user to call 911 or seek emergency care.
